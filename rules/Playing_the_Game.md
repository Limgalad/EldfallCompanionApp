# Playing the Game

## Game Setup

### Quest Selection

First, select a Quest with the other players. The Quest sets the circumstances, goal, end conditions and other variables.

There is a variety of Quests that you can choose from. You can find the full collection of Quests on the webpage, eldfall-chronicles.com under "Resources".

### Determine the Game Size

The Game Size defines the amount of Recruitment Points each player has for assembling and outfitting their Party. Each model has its own Recruitment Cost.

Hint: The recommended Game Size for starting players is 60 pts (this normally allows recruiting 3 models with some spare pts for Upgrades per player), while we encourage players to try out larger games as well (60-90 pts or more).

### Creating a Party

A party consists of a group of models that belong to a single player. Each model belongs to a specific Faction displayed on their Model Profile.

- Choose a Faction, then choose the models to employ.
- A player may only enlist models from the chosen Faction along with Neutral models.
- At least 1 enlisted model must be affiliated with the selected Faction.
- A player may choose the Neutral Faction, in which case they may only enlist models with Neutral affiliation and purchase only Neutral Upgrades.
- The number of enlisted models of the same name must not exceed their Limit.
- A single party may contain up to 10 models.
- Players create their parties in private.

### Leader

After assembling your party, you must assign the status of Leader to one model. As long as the Leader is not Incapacitated or Dead, the player may use Stratagems during their Strategic Phase.

Hint: Because only the Leader may use Stratagems, choosing a model with Stratagems on its profile is beneficial.

### Upgrades

When creating a party, you may purchase Upgrades and assign them to models during Deployment. Upgrades are purchased with remaining Recruitment Points.

- The combined point value of party members and their Upgrades must not exceed the Game Size.
- A player may purchase only Neutral Upgrades and Upgrades belonging to their chosen Faction.
- Upgrades with the same name may be purchased up to their specified Limit.
- A player may assign up to 1 Upgrade per model.
- A model cannot be assigned more than one Upgrade with the same name.

### Field Setup

The table, or rather, the field may be set up according to the players' preferences. Any terrain can be used. We encourage the use of home-made terrain.

For standard play, we recommend the use of 3D scenery (obstacles of different heights and widths) and a field size of 36x36 inches. We recommend this size to have the most balance between ranged and melee combat, when a player plays a party of 3 to 5 models.

Note: Different field setups affect gameplay in different ways. Keep in mind that the larger the field, the farther models will need to travel to engage in melee combat, while ranged models may gain an advantage. Likewise, on a smaller field, melee models will be able to close the distance more quickly and may therefore have the advantage.

Different field setups affect the gameplay in different ways. The balance between melee and ranged combat is affected by different scenery object layouts, by the altitude of the scenery pieces, by the density of the terrain, by the amount and type of cover etc.

You can choose to apply Environments to your terrain setup. It can be fun to experiment with your setup, so don't be afraid to try out different arrangements.

### Player Count (Optional)

The core rules are written mainly with two players in mind; however, they are generally applicable to solo and multiplayer play.

If you are playing alone (solo) or if you are playing with more than two players (multiplayer), please refer to the Solo and Multiplayer supplementary rules, which can be found online at eldfall-chronicles.com under "Resources".

These supplementary rules are mandatory for participation in tournaments, competitions, and other organized play.

### Format of Play (Optional)

**Rivalry:** Party creation as per core rules.

**Guildsworn:** In this format, you assume the role of a member of the Adventurers' Guild. Affiliation is not important. The player may form a party from any faction regardless of Affiliation. All models have a Limit of 1. Authority Stratagems affect all of your party's models regardless of Affiliation (but not an allied player's party).

## Initiative Check

After the field is set up, all players perform an Initiative Check. Players roll a single die and add the Party Leaders' Morale value to the result. The player with the higher sum wins the initiative and may choose one of the following (the other decision is left to the other player):

- **Turn Order:** Choose who will be the Active Player first (1st Player). Players deploy models in the same order. (If there are multiple players, they may determine their order according to their Initiative Check results.)
- **Deployment Position:** Choose the Deployment zone to place models. Furthermore, they may decide whether the players will deploy their entire parties at once or interchangeably deploy one model after the other. (If there are multiple players, they can choose their position according to their Initiative Check results.)

After all players have deployed their models, they reveal their party list to each other.

## Deployment

Players deploy their models fully within their Deployment zones unless specified otherwise.

- Deployment zones are imaginary belts on the field measured from the opposing edges, commonly 8 inches wide (may vary depending on the Quest).
- A model can only be deployed on a surface where at least half of its base fully fits.
- A model may be deployed in the Crouched State, Flying State, or Mounted.

After all the players deploy their models, the game can begin.
