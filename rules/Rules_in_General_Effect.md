# Rules in General Effect

## Advanced Rules

You can find advanced rules on the webpage: eldfall-chronicles.com. Look under the "Resources" subpage. They add variety, tactical depth, and immersion to the gameplay for more advanced players. New advanced rules will be added with future expansions of the game.

## Broken Morale

If at any time the number of Alive models in a party falls below half of their original number, Broken Morale is triggered. The models of that party become affected by the **Panicked State** until the end of the game. (If the number of models rises above half again, this rule ceases to be in effect.)

> **Example:** In a party of three, when two members fall, the one remaining becomes Panicked. In a party of four, when three fall, the remaining one becomes panicked. In a party of five, when three fall, the remaining two become panicked, etc.

## Characters

Models with a specific name are Characters. Characters cannot be assigned Upgrades when creating your party. For a list of all Characters, see the FAQ.

## End of the Game

Different Quests have different endgame conditions, however, below are some universal rules:

- Depending on the Quest, a game may end when specific endgame conditions stated in the Quest are met.
- The Quest automatically ends if, at the beginning of their turn, a player has all models in either the Incapacitated or Dead State.
- Unless specified otherwise, the game ends after the last player concludes their 5th turn.
- The victorious player is the player who scored the greater amount of **Victory Points (VP)**.
- The defeated players are the players with the fewer amount of VP.
- If all players have the same amount of VP, the game ends in a draw.
- Unless stated otherwise, the maximum number of VP a player can obtain in a single Quest is 10.

## Friendly Fire

If a model delivers a Hit that could deal damage to an allied model, that allied model must perform a Morale roll. On failure, that allied model becomes **Panicked** until its next Strategic Phase.

> **Example 1:** A model declares an Attack, that can cause Wounds and has an Area of Effect (AoE). Incidentally, one of its allied models gets caught in the AoE and suffers a Hit from the Attack. To avoid becoming Panicked, that allied model must perform a successful Morale roll.

> **Example 2:** A model declares a Ranged Attack targeting an enemy model in Base Contact with an allied model. On rolls of 17, 18, 19, 20 the ally is Hit. To avoid becoming Panicked, that allied model must perform a successful Morale roll.

## Monster Factions

Certain Factions do not coexist with civilized Factions. Monster Factions cannot hire Neutral models when creating a party and may only use Neutral Upgrades and Neutral Schemes. Currently, the **Oni Clans** and **Goblin Wartribes** are Monster Factions.

## Newer Editions of the Game's Rules

Any changes to the rules, profiles of models, or important documents will appear on our webpage, eldfall-chronicles.com under "Resources", and will be featured in newer editions of the game. These documents take priority over any past editions and their use is mandatory for participation in tournaments, competitions and organized play. The use of out-dated documents is, however, allowed in casual play.

## Priority Over the Core Rules

Abilities (Skills, Traits, Combat Arts, Spellcrafts), Spells, Items, Upgrades, Class-specific Abilities, Stratagems, and certain Effects take priority over the core rules and may override their restrictions. Rules stated on cards or within Spells take precedence.

## Recovery Check

During its own Strategic Phase, an Incapacitated model may perform a Toughness roll:

- **On success:** The model regains 1 HP and gains the Weakened State.
- **On failure:** The model remains Incapacitated.

An Incapacitated model with an allied model in Base Contact (where neither model is Engaged) may reroll the Recovery Check once.
